Help & Support

Get answers to common questions about the placement process.

How do I apply to a drive?

Go to Placement Drives, check your eligibility badge, and click Apply. You must upload a resume first.

How is my ATS score calculated?

Our AI analyzer checks resume structure, keyword density, action verbs and formatting to estimate how ATS software would parse your resume.

Who do I contact for placement queries?

Reach the CDPC office at cdpc@rguktong.ac.in or visit the placement cell in the admin block.

How do I update my CGPA or profile info?

Use the profile menu (top right) → Edit Profile to update your academic and contact details anytime.

About

RGUKT Ongole Career Development & Placement Cell.

RGUKT Ongole (Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies, Ongole Campus) is one of five IIIT campuses under RGUKT, Andhra Pradesh, established to provide free, high-quality technical education to meritorious rural students. The Career Development and Placement Cell (CDPC) coordinates campus recruitment, internships, and skill-development activities for students.

This Placement Management System is an academic project built to digitize and streamline the entire placement lifecycle — from resume building and drive applications to interview scheduling and result publication — for students, the CDPC/administration, and recruiting companies.
