Help & Support

Guide for recruiting on the RGUKT Ongole portal.

How do I post a new drive?

Go to My Drives → Post New Drive. Your posting will be reviewed and made visible by CDPC.

How do I shortlist a candidate?

Open Applicants, filter by drive, and update the status dropdown next to each candidate.

Can I browse students who haven't applied yet?

Yes — use Browse Students to filter the full eligible pool by CGPA and branch.

About

RGUKT Ongole Career Development & Placement Cell.

RGUKT Ongole is one of five IIIT campuses under Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies, Andhra Pradesh. The CDPC facilitates campus placements connecting recruiting companies with talented engineering graduates across Computer Science, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering branches.
