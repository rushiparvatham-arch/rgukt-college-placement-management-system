Welcome to the Placement Portal

At RGUKT Ongole, we nurture the top 1% of rural engineering minds through our rigorous 6-year integrated B.Tech curriculum. Driven by academic excellence, hands-on technical proficiency, our students are systematically prepared to step seamlessly into the global technology ecosystem.

Our placement portal serves as a unified, transparent gateway connecting industry leaders with future-ready engineering innovators.

For Recruiters

Discover Unmatched Potential

Gain access to a highly competitive, pre-screened talent pool specializing in CSE, ECE, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering. Our students undergo an extensive 2-year foundational pre-university phase followed by 4 years of intensive engineering specialization. This unique pipeline ensures deep logical reasoning, sharp analytical skills, and resilient problem-solving capabilities.
