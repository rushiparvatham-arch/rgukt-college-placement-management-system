Help & Support

Admin guide for the CDPC portal.

How do I create a new drive?

Go to Drive Management → New Drive, select the company, and set eligibility criteria (min CGPA, max backlogs, branches).

How do I move a student through the pipeline?

Update the application status in Applications, or schedule an interview directly from Interview Management.

How do I publish final results?

Use Results → Publish Result, choose the application and mark Selected / Rejected / Waitlisted with remarks.

About

RGUKT Ongole Career Development & Placement Cell.

The CDPC (Career Development and Placement Cell) at RGUKT Ongole coordinates all recruitment activity for the campus — from onboarding companies and running eligibility checks, to scheduling interviews and publishing final placement outcomes. This admin portal centralizes those workflows into a single dashboard.
